### 1. Key Benefits  
1. **Hospitalization Coverage** – The insurer will pay for medical expenses incurred due to hospitalization, provided the treatment is advised by a qualified medical practitioner.  
2. **Annual Renewal** – The policy remains in force as long as the policyholder renews it each year and pays the premium on time.  
3. **Free‑Look Period** – A 15‑day free‑look period is offered, allowing the policyholder to review the contract and cancel without penalty if dissatisfied.  

### 2. Exclusions  
1. **Pre‑Existing Diseases** – Any treatment for a pre‑existing condition is **not covered** for the first 48 months from the policy start date.  
2. **Dental Treatments** – All dental treatments and surgeries are expressly excluded from coverage.  

### 3. General Conditions (Important to Note)  
1. **Premium Payment** – Premium must be paid before the due date each policy year to keep the coverage active.  
2. **Renewal Requirement** – The policyholder must renew the policy annually; lapse of renewal results in loss of coverage.  
3. **Contractual Basis** – The document is a contract of insurance between SBI General Insurance Company Limited and the policyholder, as stated in the preamble.
